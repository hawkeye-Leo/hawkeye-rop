Qt 5.15
-------
HawkeyeRop.exe is built against Qt 5.15 (MSVC 2019 64-bit) and, in a normal
deployment, links dynamically to Qt shared libraries (for example Qt5Core.dll,
Qt5Gui.dll, Qt5Widgets.dll, and the platforms/ plugin directory).

Copyright (C) The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors.
Licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 (LGPL-3.0).

Qt is used as a separate shared library. Per LGPL-3.0, you may replace the Qt
DLLs next to HawkeyeRop.exe with another compatible Qt 5.15 build for
Windows x64.
